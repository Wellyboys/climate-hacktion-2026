Why we chose this topic

The material exists.
The missing piece is coordination.

Our project starts with an Auckland baseline of approximately 2 million tonnes of excavated soil annually. Matching material, quantity, timing and acceptance criteria early creates a practical pathway to local reuse.

Data, assumptions & map sources

Scope: Auckland only. The repository provides an annual scenario, not site-level or nationwide measurements. Comparison columns are anchored together near Auckland for readability; their footprints do not represent project sites. No district-level soil values are inferred.

Calculation: annual soil = 2,000,000 × 1.012^(year − 2026). Reuse share rises linearly from 1% in 2026 to 10% in 2035. Disposal route: 92 km; reuse route: 12 km; emissions factor: 0.105 kg CO₂-e / tonne-km. Avoided transport emissions = reused tonnes × (92 − 12) × 0.105 / 1,000. This covers transport only. The 15% global benchmark in the source is not the assumed ReGround target.
